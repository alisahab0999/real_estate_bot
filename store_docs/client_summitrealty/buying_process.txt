Buying Process at Summit Realty Group

Step 1: Get Pre-Approved. Before touring homes, we recommend getting pre-approved with a lender so you know your budget. We can refer you to a few local lenders on request, but you're welcome to use your own.

Step 2: Tour Properties. Once you have a budget in mind, an agent will set up showings for properties that match your criteria. You can request a showing anytime through this chat, and an agent will personally confirm the appointment.

Step 3: Make an Offer. When you find a property you want, your agent will help you prepare a written offer, including price, contingencies, and closing timeline. Offers are submitted directly to the listing agent or seller.

Step 4: Inspection and Appraisal. After an accepted offer, buyers typically have 7-10 days to complete a home inspection. Your lender will also order an appraisal to confirm the home's value supports the loan amount.

Step 5: Closing. Closing typically takes 30-45 days from an accepted offer, depending on financing. You'll review and sign final paperwork with a title company, and funds are transferred at closing.

Earnest Money. Buyers typically submit earnest money (usually 1% of the purchase price) within 3 days of an accepted offer. This is credited toward your down payment at closing and is refundable under agreed contingencies.

Working With an Agent. All our agents work on your behalf as buyer's representatives. There is no cost to you as a buyer — commission is customarily paid by the seller as part of the transaction.
