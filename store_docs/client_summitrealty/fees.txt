Fees at Summit Realty Group

For Buyers. There is no fee to work with a Summit Realty buyer's agent. Our commission is paid by the seller as part of the transaction, standard for most residential sales in this market.

For Sellers. Listing commission is 6% of the final sale price, split between the listing agent and buyer's agent, and is only owed if the property successfully sells (paid at closing).

For Renters. A $50 non-refundable application fee is charged per applicant to cover credit and background checks. If approved, a security deposit equal to one month's rent is due at lease signing along with the first month's rent. Some units may require a pet deposit — see our pet policy document for details.

Closing Costs (Buyers). Typical buyer closing costs range from 2-3% of the purchase price and cover items like loan origination fees, title insurance, appraisal, and prepaid property taxes/insurance. Your lender will provide an itemized closing disclosure before closing.

Home Warranty. Optional and not required. Some sellers offer a one-year home warranty as part of the sale; ask your agent whether a specific listing includes one.

Late Rent Fee. For our managed rental properties, rent is due on the 1st of the month, with a grace period through the 3rd. A late fee applies starting on the 4th — the exact amount is specified in each lease agreement.
