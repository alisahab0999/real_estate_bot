Rental Application Requirements at Summit Realty Group

To apply for one of our rental listings, you'll need to submit the following:

1. Completed rental application form (available from your agent or on request).
2. Government-issued photo ID for each applicant 18 or older.
3. Proof of income: your two most recent pay stubs, or an offer letter if you're starting a new job, or two years of tax returns if self-employed. We generally look for gross monthly income of at least three times the monthly rent.
4. Employment verification: your agent may contact your employer to confirm your position and length of employment.
5. Two most recent months of bank statements.
6. Rental history: contact information for your current and previous landlord, if applicable.
7. Application fee: a non-refundable $50 per applicant, used to cover the cost of a credit and background check.

Processing Time. Applications are typically processed within 2-3 business days once all required documents are submitted. We evaluate every application using the same criteria applied consistently to all applicants: income, credit history, rental history, and background check results.

Co-Signers. If an applicant does not independently meet the income requirement, a co-signer may be added. The co-signer must submit the same documentation and typically needs to show gross monthly income of at least four times the monthly rent.

Security Deposit. Security deposits are equal to one month's rent for most units and are due at lease signing, along with the first month's rent.
