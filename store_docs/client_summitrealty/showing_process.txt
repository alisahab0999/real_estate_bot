Showing Process at Summit Realty Group

Requesting a Showing. You can request a showing anytime by providing the property address, your name, email, phone number, and your preferred date and time. Once submitted, this is logged as a request — an agent will personally reach out to confirm the actual appointment, since availability depends on the current occupant or seller's schedule.

Confirmation Timing. Most showing requests are confirmed within 24 hours. If you don't hear back within that window, feel free to follow up.

Rescheduling or Cancelling. If you need to reschedule or cancel a showing, contact your assigned agent directly, or submit a new showing request with your updated preferred time and mention it's a reschedule.

What to Bring. For rental properties, bring a photo ID. For homes for sale, no documentation is required to tour — just yourself and anyone else who'll be part of the decision.

Group Showings. Open houses, when scheduled, are open to the public without an appointment. All other showings are by appointment only, to respect the privacy of current occupants.

Virtual Showings. Video walkthroughs are available for some listings upon request — ask your agent whether a specific property has one available, or whether a live video tour can be arranged.
