Pet Policy at Summit Realty Group

Pets are allowed at select rental properties, marked "pet-friendly" in the listing. Pet policies vary by property owner, so always confirm with your agent before applying if you have a pet.

Pet Deposit. Where pets are allowed, a refundable pet deposit of $300 per pet is typically required, in addition to the standard security deposit. This is refunded at move-out, less any pet-related damage.

Pet Rent. Some properties charge an additional $25-$40 per month in pet rent, added to the monthly rent amount. This will be specified in the lease.

Breed and Weight Restrictions. Some properties have breed or weight restrictions set by the property owner or insurance carrier. Your agent can confirm whether a specific listing has restrictions before you apply.

Number of Pets. Most pet-friendly properties allow up to 2 pets per unit; a few allow more. This varies by property.

Service and Assistance Animals. Service animals and assistance animals are not considered pets under fair housing law and are not subject to pet deposits, pet rent, or breed/weight restrictions. If you require a reasonable accommodation for a service or assistance animal, let your agent know and we'll handle the request accordingly.

For-Sale Properties. Pet policies do not apply to properties for sale — buyers may keep pets as they wish, subject to any applicable HOA rules, which vary by community.
