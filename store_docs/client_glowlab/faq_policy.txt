Frequently Asked Questions

Are your products cruelty-free?
Yes, all GlowLab products are cruelty-free and never tested on animals.

Can I use multiple products together?
Yes, most of our products can be layered. We recommend applying lightest
formulas first (serums) before heavier ones (creams), and always apply
sunscreen last in your morning routine.

Are your products safe during pregnancy?
We recommend consulting your doctor before starting any new skincare
routine during pregnancy, as some active ingredients may not be
recommended.

Do you test for allergies?
Our products go through standard formulation safety testing, but we
always recommend a patch test before full use if you have sensitive skin
or known allergies.
