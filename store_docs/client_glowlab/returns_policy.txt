Returns Policy

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery for unopened products. Due to
hygiene reasons, opened skincare products cannot be returned unless
defective.

To start a return, email support@glowlabskincare.com with your order
number and reason for return.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business
days after we receive the returned item.

If a product causes an allergic reaction or arrives damaged, contact us
within 14 days with photos for a full refund or replacement, no time
limit restriction applies to defective items.
